Let's check on your device

Reclaim City looks over your Mac, Windows PC, or Linux machine for signs of trouble — unwanted software, risky settings, and things worth double-checking. It explains what it finds in plain language, so you can decide what to do next.

🔒
Read-only by default. Reclaim City only looks — it never changes anything on your device without your explicit OK.
