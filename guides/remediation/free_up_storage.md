The scan added up space taken by things you may not need any more: old
downloads, disk images, the Trash, and large temporary storage folders. None
of it was deleted. These steps let you choose what to remove yourself. If you
are unsure about a file or folder, leave it alone.

## Step 1: Look at what is using the space

Open System Settings > General > Storage. It lists what is using space on
your Mac and offers its own suggestions. The other notes from this scan name
the folders it measured.

## Step 2: Empty the Trash

Files in the Trash still take up space. Open Finder, right-click the Trash in
the Dock, and choose Empty Trash. Check first that nothing in it is something
you still want.

## Step 3: Clear out old downloads and disk images

Open your Downloads folder in Finder. Old installers (files ending in .dmg)
are usually safe to delete once the app is installed. Move anything you want
to keep somewhere else first.

## Step 4: Leave app folders to the apps

Large folders in ~/Library belong to the apps that made them. Remove them only
from inside the app (for example, a "clear cache" setting) or by uninstalling
an app you no longer use. Deleting them by hand can break the app.
