## Step 1: Make your passwords and sign-ins stronger, at your own pace

The emergency is over; this part can take days. Reclaim City, a free game from
the same team, teaches it: it walks you through new passwords and two-step
sign-in one account at a time, with your paper list from Phase 3 to hand. Open
play.multiversegames.ai/reclaim-city and pick the "My email got hacked" Quick
Quest, then carry on to the other districts. You don't need an account, and
your progress stays in your browser.

## Step 2: Review app permissions on your phone

Go through installed apps and revoke location, contacts, and microphone
access for anything that doesn't need it.
