## Step 1: Change passwords on the accounts that matter most

From a device you trust, and after your email, in this order: your password
manager (its main password, and sign out devices you don't recognize), any
account where you saw something strange in Phase 1, your phone's account
(Apple Account or Google: remove devices you don't recognize), then social
media and messaging, banking, cloud storage and your main work account. For
each: a new password of its own, sign out other devices, and look for posts or
messages you didn't send. The toolkit does not inspect passwords.

## Step 2: Contact your bank and financial institutions

Look through recent payments on each bank account and card. Call the
institutions tied to any account where you saw suspicious activity, on the
number from the back of your card or their own website, never one from a
message. Ask them to flag the account and review recent transactions with you.
Also call your mobile carrier and ask them to confirm that nobody has set up
forwarding of your texts.

## Step 3: Write down your progress

Keep a paper list (not digital, in case the device is still a concern) of
what you noticed and when, and every account you've secured so far. You'll want
this in Phase 4, and anyone helping you will too. If money or your identity was
involved, the identity theft recovery profile is next.
