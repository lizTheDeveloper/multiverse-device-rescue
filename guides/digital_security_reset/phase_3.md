## Step 1: Change passwords on the accounts that matter most

From a device you trust, change the password on any account where you saw
something strange in Phase 1, then banking, cloud storage and your main work
account. Give each one its own new password. The toolkit does not inspect
passwords.

## Step 2: Contact your bank and financial institutions

Call the institutions tied to any account where you saw suspicious
activity in Phase 1. Ask them to flag the account and review recent
transactions with you.

## Step 3: Write down your progress

Keep a paper list (not digital, in case the device is still a concern) of
every account you've secured so far. You'll want this in Phase 4.
