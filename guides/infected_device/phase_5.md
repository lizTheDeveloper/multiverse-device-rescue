## Step 1: Change passwords from a different, clean device

Anything typed on the infected computer may have been recorded. Change your
passwords from a different device you trust (another computer, or your
phone), never from the infected one, even after it's been cleaned. Start
with your email, because it can reset everything else.

## Step 2: Follow the accounts walkthrough

The accounts walkthrough (passwords, two-step sign-in, signing out other
sessions) is the Digital Security Reset: `rescue guide
digital_security_reset`, or the "I think I've been hacked" worry in the app.
Open it on the clean device.
