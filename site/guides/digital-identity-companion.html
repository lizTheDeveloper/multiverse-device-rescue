Day Companion —

Tonight you are the attacker. You will read pretext messages and name the tell that gives them away. You will run your own account recovery and discover how far a stranger gets. Then you will close the gap. Three scorecards, three skills: classify a pretext, falsify your own recovery, and configure recovery that survives losing the device. The guides linked below are your field manuals — written for people whose accounts are already compromised, whose partners tracked their location, whose sources need protecting. Your job tonight is simpler: find your own weak point before someone else does.
