How it works

1

Open it up

Launch Device Rescue. It doesn't need an account, a subscription, or an internet connection to get started.

2

It looks around

It checks things like storage space, background apps, and common security settings — quietly, and without changing anything.

3

You decide

You'll see a plain-English summary. If something needs fixing, Device Rescue explains it and waits for your go-ahead.

Good to know

  • Works on macOS with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) today; a Windows version is coming soon.
