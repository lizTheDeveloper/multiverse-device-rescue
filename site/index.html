◈ Device Rescue

A calm checkup for your computer.

Device Rescue looks around your Mac, tells you in plain English what it finds, and never changes anything unless you say so.

🔒
Read-only by default. You're always in control.

Device Rescue only looks — it doesn't fix, delete, or change anything on its own. If it finds something worth fixing, it explains what that means and asks for your okay before doing anything. Nothing happens behind your back.

⌘ Download for Mac Apple Silicon · macOS

The download is fairly large — around 350–500 MB, because it includes everything Device Rescue needs to run on its own. It's ready to use on macOS with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) today. A Windows version is on the way.

How it works

1

Open it up

Launch Device Rescue. It doesn't need an account, a subscription, or an internet connection to get started.

2

It looks around

It checks things like storage space, background apps, and common security settings — quietly, and without changing anything.

3

You decide

You'll see a plain-English summary. If something needs fixing, Device Rescue explains it and waits for your go-ahead.
